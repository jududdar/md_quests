# Translocator Jorbin

[Translocator Jorbin](/npc/1501020) is a level 1 Gnome Warrior that spawns in [Timorous Deep](/zone/96).

## Dialog

**You say:** `Hail`


>**Translocator Jorbin says:** Hello there, Soandso. There seem to be some strange problems with the boats in this area. The Academy of Arcane Sciences has sent a small team of us to investigate them. In the meantime, I can transport you to my companion there if you wish to [travel to Oasis].

**You say:** `oasis`


**Translocator Jorbin casts:** [Portal to Oasis](/spell/2286) on target.

>**Translocator Jorbin says:** Off you go!
