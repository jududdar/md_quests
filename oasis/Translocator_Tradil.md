# Translocator Tradil

[Translocator Tradil](/npc/1501021) is a level 1 Gnome Warrior that spawns in [Oasis of Marr](/zone/37).

## Dialog
**You say:** `Hail`

>**Translocator Tradil says:** Hello there, Soandso. There seem to be some strange problems with the boats in this area. The Academy of Arcane Sciences has sent a small team of us to investigate them. In the meantime, I can transport you to my companion there if you wish to [travel to Timorous Deep].
**You say:** `timorous deep`

**Translocator Tradil casts:** [Portal to Timorous](/spell/2291) on target.
>**Translocator Tradil says:** Off you go!
