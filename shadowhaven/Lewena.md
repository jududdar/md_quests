# Lewena

[Lewena](/npc/150214) is a level 15 Wood Elf Warrior that spawns in [Shadow Haven](/zone/150).

Their primary faction is [House of Fordel](/faction/1510).



## Dialog

**You say:** `hail`


>**Lewena says:** Hi there Soandso. Care to buy me a drink? If you buy me my favorite I will tell you a secret.
